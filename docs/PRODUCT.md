# PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

## Primary entry points
- DIAGNOSE MY DAMAGE — system discovers what the visitor needs.
- ENTER THE INDEX — visitor knows approximately what they want.
- SURPRISE ME — no thought required.

## Main routes
- `/` homepage
- `/diagnose` eight-question diagnosis
- `/index` all 125 documented cases
- `/case/[id]` individual case file
- `/surprise` random eligible treatment

## Case model
case ID · title · primary damage · secondary damage · diagnosis label · intensity · symptoms · dosage · prognosis · nine emotional vectors · Spotify URL

## Distribution built into the model
shareable Damage Reports · direct case URLs · direct damage-family URLs · social cards based on diagnosis · seasonal reports · NEW CASE RELEASED archive storytelling.
