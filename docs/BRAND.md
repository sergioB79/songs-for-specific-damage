# SSD BRAND CONSTITUTION v1

## Official name
**SONGS FOR SPECIFIC DAMAGE**

## Official descriptor
**An Index of Bad Ideas & Good Music**

Do not create a second public-facing brand name.

## Core pitch
**Music for whatever is specifically wrong with you today.**

Expanded: Songs for Specific Damage is a music discovery platform that recommends playlists based on emotional situations instead of genres.

Media/social: **Spotify asks what music you like. We ask what went wrong.**

## Product thesis
**Genres describe music. Damage describes why you need it.**

## Voice
Deadpan. Specific. Administrative. Never desperate to be funny. Never explain the joke.

Recurring vocabulary: SSD//00125 · AUDIO PSYCHOLOGY UNIT · DAMAGE INDEX · CASE FILE · RECOMMENDED TREATMENT · UNLICENSED EMOTIONAL DIAGNOSTICS · CLASSIFICATION DISPUTED · DAMAGE CONFIRMED · LISTENING ADVISED · CASE REMAINS OPEN · NO GENRES CONSULTED · RESULTS MAY VARY AFTER MIDNIGHT.

## Design principles
1. **The content may be absurd. The interface may not.**
2. Weirdness must look intentional.
3. Deadpan beats punchline.
4. Every strange object needs visual authority.
5. Never decorate empty space just because it is empty.

## Experience goal
**WTF → curiosity → click → good music.**
